About OntarioBonusTaxCalc.ca

Part of the calc-hq.ca Ontario payroll calculator network

What This Tool Is

OntarioBonusTaxCalc.ca is a free Ontario bonus tax withholding estimator. It implements the CRA T4032-ON annualization method — the same methodology payroll software uses to determine how much income tax, CPP, and EI your employer withholds from a bonus payment.

The calculator is designed for Ontario employment income only. It covers tax year 2026 rates and thresholds, sourced directly from CRA T4032-ON, ESDC, and CRA CPP rate tables.

What This Tool Is Not

This is not a T1 tax return calculator. It estimates employer withholding — not your actual annual tax liability. Withholding may be higher or lower than what you ultimately owe, and the difference is reconciled at tax filing time each spring.

This tool does not constitute tax advice. For personal tax planning, RRSP strategy, or complex employment situations, consult a qualified CPA or tax professional.

Why We Built It

Most Canadians receiving a performance bonus or annual bonus are surprised by how much is withheld. The correct answer — that the CRA annualization method applies your marginal rate to the entire bonus in one pay period — requires a calculator, not a blog post. We built this tool to give Ontario employees a transparent, formula-driven, source-backed estimate of what to expect.
