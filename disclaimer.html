Estimation Only

This calculator models the CRA T4032-ON annualization method — the standard approach Canadian payroll systems use to estimate employer bonus withholding. Results are estimates. Actual withholding may differ based on:

  • Your employer's specific payroll software and rounding conventions
  • TD1 and TD1ON claim codes filed with your employer
  • RRSP deduction authorizations in effect at the time of payment
  • Year-to-date CPP and EI contribution amounts (timing-sensitive)
  • Other deductions or credits your employer has on file
  • Changes to CRA T4032 tables mid-year

Not a T1 Return Calculator

This tool calculates employer withholding on a bonus — not your actual annual income tax owing. Your true tax liability is determined on your T1 General return based on your total income, credits, and deductions for the full calendar year. A refund or balance owing at filing does not mean this calculator was incorrect — it reflects the difference between estimated withholding and actual annual tax.

Ontario Employment Income Only

This calculator applies to Ontario employment income. It does not cover Quebec (QPP, Revenu Québec), self-employment income, investment income, pension income, or any other income type. Results for employees in provinces other than Ontario are not valid.

Rate Accuracy

Rates and thresholds are sourced from official CRA and ESDC publications for tax year 2026. We update these values when new official data is published. However, we cannot guarantee that this tool reflects CRA mid-year corrections, late-published provincial adjustments, or changes that post-date our last update (January 2026). Always verify critical payroll decisions against official CRA T4032-ON tables.
