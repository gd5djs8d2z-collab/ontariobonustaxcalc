Privacy Policy

Effective date: January 1, 2026  ·  OntarioBonusTaxCalc.ca

Overview

OntarioBonusTaxCalc.ca is a free, static web tool. All calculations are performed locally in your browser using JavaScript. No salary, bonus, or tax input data is transmitted to any server, stored, logged, or shared with any third party.

Data We Collect

We do not collect personally identifiable information. The only data collected is standard web analytics — page views, approximate location (country/region level), referring source, and browser type — through Google Analytics 4 (GA4). This data is aggregated and anonymous.

Cookies

This site uses cookies set by Google for the following purposes:

  • Google Analytics (GA4): Measures aggregate traffic and usage patterns. Cookies: _ga, _ga_*. Data retention: 2 years (configurable).
  • Google AdSense: Serves display advertising. Cookies may include IDE, DSID, and related identifiers. Google uses these for interest-based ad personalization where permitted.

You can opt out of Google interest-based advertising via Google Ad Settings or install the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

Calculator Inputs

All values entered into the calculator (salary, bonus amount, pay frequency, RRSP deduction, additional withholding) are processed exclusively in your browser's memory using JavaScript. These values are never transmitted to any server, API, or third party. Refreshing or closing the page clears all inputs.

Third-Party Services

  • Google Analytics 4 — analytics.google.com — Google Privacy Policy
  • Google AdSense — pagead2.googlesyndication.com — Google Advertising Policies
  • Google Fonts (IBM Plex Sans / IBM Plex Mono) — fonts.googleapis.com — Font files may be loaded from Google servers; IP address logged per Google's infrastructure policies.

Children's Privacy

This site is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as the site evolves. The effective date at the top of this page reflects the most recent update.
